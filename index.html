Help & Tips

Camera permission

Kira only requests camera access after you tap Start Camera unless iPhone already reports permission as granted.

Saving to Photos

iPhone does not let a web app silently insert media into Photos. Kira can queue your captures so you can send a batch to the native save/share sheet.

Where is my media?

Everything captured in Kira is stored locally in Rolls. New installs start with no named rolls; media goes to Unfiled until you create one.

Keep Kira smooth

Use Smooth video quality, keep the camera in continuous-shoot mode, and avoid filling device storage completely.
